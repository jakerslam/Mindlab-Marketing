# Mindlab Solutions Agency and Service Guide

Brand Strategy Content and Paid Advertising

November 5 2026

Mindlab Solutions helps B2B and B2C businesses clarify their positioning, create useful content, and run focused advertising campaigns. Our work connects the way a business presents itself with the customers it wants to reach and the actions it wants them to take.

This guide defines Mindlab's services, industry focus, proposed packages and pricing, client responsibilities, and approach to delivery. It also establishes a direction for the agency website and initial outreach.

## Agency positioning

**Mindlab helps businesses build a clearer brand, communicate their value, and reach the right customers through strategy, content, and paid advertising.**

We work with business owners, founders, and small marketing teams who need a clear plan and consistent execution. Each engagement starts with a defined audience, offer, objective, and scope.

## Core services

**Brand strategy and planning.** Audience definition, competitor review, positioning, value proposition, messaging, brand voice, content themes, and practical marketing plans.

**Content development.** Interview-based writing, social content, articles, customer stories, case study development, email copy, and campaign messaging. The selected package defines the formats and volume included.

**Paid advertising.** Campaign planning, audience or keyword research, ad concepts and copy, campaign setup, optimization, and reporting against an agreed conversion goal.

## Who is a good fit

Mindlab is designed for businesses with a specific offer, a reachable audience, a responsible decision-maker, and enough time and budget to support delivery. Clients provide access to their expertise, approved assets, and timely feedback.

Brand strategy can support an established business or a business preparing to launch. Paid campaigns require a validated offer, a working purchase or inquiry path, appropriate measurement, and capacity to handle demand.

## How delivery changes by audience

**For B2B clients**, work may emphasize expertise, buyer education, evidence, and support for a sales conversation. Campaigns can be evaluated through qualified inquiries, held meetings, opportunities, and eventual customers.

**For B2C clients**, work may emphasize the customer benefit, experience, offer, and a clear path to purchase or booking. Measurement follows the business model: appointments, quote requests, purchases, or repeat customers.

One business may serve both audiences. Its scope must specify which audience and offer the engagement covers.

## Initial market focus

Keep both B2B and B2C eligible. Begin outreach with one specific segment from each group where Mindlab has relevant relationships, useful examples, or a clear delivery advantage. Compare buyer interest, budget fit, access to source material, and delivery effort before expanding.

For ecommerce work, confirm the client's store, product assets, measurement, and fulfillment process before accepting a campaign. Catalog management, feeds, store development, and technical integrations receive separate scopes.

## Brand Blueprint

**Proposed fee:** $1,250 per project. **Typical delivery:** two to three weeks after required inputs and stakeholder interviews are complete.

This engagement establishes how a business should describe its value, whom it should prioritize, and how its marketing should support its commercial goals.

## Included deliverables

- Up to two stakeholder interviews and review of existing brand and marketing material.
- Review of three relevant competitors and their public positioning.
- One priority customer profile, including needs, buying considerations, and common objections.
- A positioning statement, value proposition, and reasons to choose the business.
- A message hierarchy covering the main promise, supporting points, and key offer language.
- Brand voice guidance with practical examples of how the business should communicate.
- Three content themes and a 90-day marketing action plan with channel roles and priorities.
- One final presentation and two consolidated revision rounds.

## What the client receives

A written strategy and messaging document that can guide website copy, content, sales conversations, and campaigns. The action plan identifies priorities, proposed measures, and the assets needed to proceed.

The project covers one business brand and one priority audience or offer. Additional brands, extensive customer research, naming programs, or multiple market strategies require an expanded scope.

## Client inputs

Business goals, service or product information, access to relevant stakeholders, existing customer insight, known competitors, available performance data, and one person responsible for consolidated approval.

## Scope boundaries

Logo creation, visual identity design, packaging, original photography, video, website implementation, ongoing content, and advertising management are separately scoped services.

## Choosing the next step

The client can use the Blueprint independently or commission Content Studio or Demand Campaigns to implement the relevant parts of the plan. Subsequent delivery is agreed in a separate scope.

## Content Studio

**Proposed fee:** $1,000 per month, plus $250 onboarding. **Recommended initial engagement:** 90 days.

This package turns the client's expertise, customer insight, and offer into a consistent body of written content. The monthly plan connects each piece to a defined audience and purpose.

## Included each month

- One source interview of up to 45 minutes with a founder, expert, or relevant team member.
- A monthly content calendar with topics, purpose, and suggested publishing dates.
- Eight written social posts for one agreed business page or individual profile.
- One article of up to 1,200 words or one email of up to 800 words.
- Two consolidated revision rounds on the monthly content batch.
- A performance review and one monthly planning call.

Onboarding covers the audience and voice brief, review of existing material, the selected profile or page, source collection, and the approval workflow.

## B2B and B2C applications

For B2B, the content may include expert insights, buyer education, an evidence-backed case study, or responses to common sales objections. For B2C, it may include service or product education, customer stories, frequently asked questions, and promotional messaging.

The article or email is selected during planning. Case studies rely on available evidence, appropriate permissions, and the normal word and revision limits. Fresh customer interviews or extensive research require separate scoping.

## Publishing and production

The standard package supplies approved written content ready for the agreed publishing workflow. Publishing through authorized client accounts can be included when specified in the proposal; this does not increase the number of profiles or deliverables.

The client supplies usable visual assets and factual review. Graphic design, photography, video production, daily community engagement, direct-message management, email distribution, automation, additional channels, and additional content are quoted separately.

One post means one written piece for the agreed channel. Adaptations for another channel count as additional work. Unused monthly deliverables do not automatically roll over; any rescheduling is documented in the delivery plan.

## Demand Campaigns

**Proposed fee:** $1,250 per month, plus $500 launch. **Advertising spend:** additional. **Recommended initial engagement:** 90 days.

This package plans and manages paid campaigns around one defined product or service offer and an agreed conversion goal. The platform is selected according to the audience, offer, budget, required assets, and Mindlab's delivery capabilities.

## Included scope

- One advertising platform, one agreed market, and one product or service offer.
- Up to three active campaigns and up to $10,000 in monthly media spend under management.
- Audience or keyword research, campaign structure, and a media plan.
- Ad concepts and copy using approved client brand assets, with up to four new copy variants per month.
- A landing-page messaging brief for the agreed offer and conversion action.
- Campaign setup, launch, weekly optimization, monthly reporting, and one monthly review call.

The launch fee covers account review, the initial campaign plan and configuration, conversion-definition review, and launch coordination. It includes verification of existing standard tracking with the client's technical owner. New tracking implementations and repairs are separately scoped.

## Before a campaign goes live

The client and Mindlab agree the target audience, offer, media budget, conversion definition, and reporting measures. The client provides approved claims and assets, account access, a working destination, and a responsible sales or operations contact.

For inquiry campaigns, agree how leads will be assessed and followed up. For purchase or booking campaigns, confirm that the transaction path and measurement work before launch.

## How performance is reviewed

Lead-based campaigns track suitable inquiries, held conversations, and opportunities as reliable sales data becomes available. Purchase- or booking-based campaigns track completed actions, acquisition cost, and relevant commercial outcomes.

## Additional scope

New platforms, additional markets or offers, media spend above the management cap, custom creative production, website development, catalog or feed management, and extensive CRM or tracking integration require a separate quote. Changes to offer or creative direction after approval may require a change request.

## Working with Mindlab

Each engagement follows a consistent process, with the details recorded in the proposal and service agreement.

**Discovery and fit.** Discuss the business, audience, goals, current marketing, available assets, and budget. Identify the main problem and recommend a suitable starting engagement.

**Scope and agreement.** Confirm deliverables, responsibilities, exclusions, schedule, fees, media budget where relevant, and the approval process before work begins.

**Onboarding and kickoff.** Collect access and materials, confirm the responsible contacts, establish a baseline, and agree the first delivery milestones.

**Delivery and review.** Develop the agreed work, collect consolidated feedback, make included revisions, and publish or launch after explicit client approval.

**Measurement and next steps.** Review work delivered, performance, constraints, and recommended changes. Expansion requires an agreed scope and price.

## Commercial and collaboration terms

**Payment.** Brand Blueprint is proposed at 50% to begin and 50% before final handoff. Retainers are billed monthly in advance. Setup fees are due before onboarding or campaign preparation begins. Media is paid separately through the client's advertising account.

**Term and cancellation.** The proposed initial retainer term is 90 days, followed by monthly renewal with 30 days written notice. The signed agreement confirms renewal, cancellation, pause, and refund terms. Pausing paid media does not automatically pause the service engagement.

**Approvals and revisions.** The client appoints one approver and supplies consolidated feedback. A revision round refines work within the approved brief. A new audience, offer, deliverable, or creative direction is a scope change. Work is never published on the basis of silence.

**Communication.** Each retainer includes one monthly planning or review call. Content and Campaigns uses one coordinated call. The working proposal identifies the primary communication channel, working hours, and response expectations.

**Ownership and access.** Clients retain their platform accounts and data. Paid-for final approved deliverables transfer as specified in the agreement; third-party licenses and Mindlab's reusable methods remain subject to their own terms. Case study use requires permission.

**Client dependencies.** Late inputs or approvals can move delivery dates. Account access, factual accuracy, rights to supplied assets, sales follow-up, inventory, and fulfillment remain assigned client responsibilities in the agreement.

## Measuring success

Every engagement starts with a defined objective, baseline, and practical measures. Reporting explains what was delivered, what happened, what remains uncertain, and what should change next.

| Workstream | Measures to agree |
| --- | --- |
| Brand strategy | Approved positioning, usable message hierarchy, completed plan, and adoption in the agreed business materials |
| Content | Delivery and approval consistency, relevant audience response, engagement, useful website actions, and attributable inquiries where available |
| Lead generation | Suitable inquiries, held meetings, opportunities, acquisition cost, and eventual wins |
| Purchases and bookings | Completed transactions or appointments, acquisition cost, cancellations or refunds, and contribution after marketing |

Platform-attributed conversions and revenue should be described using the agreed attribution method. Evidence of incremental business requires more than a platform report. Commercial reviews also consider sales follow-up, pricing, buying time, and fulfillment.

## Common client questions

**Can we buy one service?** Yes. Each core package can be purchased separately. Content and Campaigns combines the two recurring scopes for clients who need coordinated execution.

**Do we need Brand Blueprint first?** It is useful when positioning or messaging needs work. Clients with a clear audience and offer can begin with content or campaigns.

**Are visuals and video included?** The standard content price covers writing. Design, photography, and video receive a separate production scope and price.

**Is ad spend included?** Media is additional to the service fee and launch fee. Mindlab and the client agree the budget before launch.

**How soon should results appear?** Brand Blueprint has a defined project schedule, and content follows a monthly calendar. Campaign outcomes depend on demand, budget, assets, the offer, and the buying process. The initial 90-day period supports structured delivery and learning; longer sales cycles require continued tracking.

**Can Mindlab guarantee a number of sales?** Sales targets are agreed using the client's baseline and economics. Mindlab commits to the contracted work and review process; sales depend on factors across marketing, the offer, sales handling, and delivery.

**Can scope grow?** Additional content, production, channels, markets, and technical work can be quoted before being added. Changes become part of the written delivery plan.

## Website and outreach direction

Mindlab's website and sales conversations should use the same package names, scope definitions, and pricing basis as this guide. The shared message can cover both B2B and B2C, supported by audience-specific examples.

## Website structure

- **Home:** explain who Mindlab helps, the problems addressed, the three core services, relevant work, the process, and the next step.
- **Services:** explain deliverables, fit, exclusions, and the working rhythm.
- **Pricing:** compare package fees, setup costs, included scope, combined delivery, and first-month estimates.
- **Industries:** explain the B2B and B2C applications and provide relevant examples for the selected markets.
- **Work and Insights:** publish permissioned projects, substantiated results, useful analysis, and clearly labeled demonstrations.
- **About:** introduce the people doing the work, their actual experience, and the working approach.
- **Book a Call:** collect name, email, company or website, primary goal, and a budget range; explain the purpose of the conversation.

Suggested homepage introduction:

**Build a clearer brand and reach the right customers.**

Mindlab Solutions brings brand strategy, content, and paid advertising together to help B2B and B2C businesses communicate their value and support their next stage of growth.

**Primary action:** Book a 30-minute strategy call.

## Outreach approach

Start with warm introductions and complementary partners, including website developers, visual designers, and business consultants. Explain the specific customer problem and the package that addresses it.

Build two initial prospect lists: 25 businesses in one B2B segment and 25 in one B2C segment. Use a real observation, a relevant example, and a small next step for each business. A positioning review can introduce Brand Blueprint; useful content angles can introduce Content Studio; campaign conversations require an offer, budget, and working conversion path.

Track contacts, qualified replies, held discovery calls, proposals, wins, objections, and expected delivery effort. Compare the two groups before expanding outreach. Follow the sending provider's rules and applicable marketing requirements, and honor declines and opt-outs.

## Launch priorities

Confirm delivery capacity and supported platforms, validate the proposed prices against costs, prepare relevant work examples, and conduct buyer interviews. Aim for one or two paid pilot clients, record actual delivery time, and refine the offer using evidence.

The initial business objective is a repeatable engagement that clients value and Mindlab can deliver profitably. Keep package scopes and commercial terms consistent across proposals, the website, and sales conversations.
