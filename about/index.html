Mindlab brings brand strategy, written content, and paid advertising together around your business goals.

Six questions for a better brief+
  1. The offer

    What are we asking people to consider?

  2. The audience

    Who needs it, and what matters to them?

  3. The next step

    What should someone do after reading?

  4. The evidence

    Which reasons to believe can we support?

  5. The constraints

    What budget, inputs, and approvals shape the work?

  6. The review

    What will we measure, and who will act on it?
