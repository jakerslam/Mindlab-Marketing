Alder & Plane Kitchens

A photo-led campaign for a fictional Denver remodeler, with a whole-kitchen transformation and two paid Instagram ads.

Brand Blueprint · Content Studio · Ad CampaignsFictional business · Original demonstration
The brief↘Creative examples↘The deliverables↘Proposed measurement↘

The brief.

Help homeowners picture a full kitchen remodel and decide whether to discuss their project.

Alder & Plane is a fictional design-build studio for full kitchen remodels in the Denver metro. Remodel conversations start at $75,000. If the project fits, paid design helps define the work before the studio prepares a construction proposal.

Sample concept. No campaign was run and no client results are claimed.

Creative examples.

A before-and-after post shows how much one room can change. The paid ads show that transformation and a second finished kitchen. The images are AI-generated concepts.

Shared ad copy

Ready for a new kitchen?

Ready to remodel the whole kitchen? Alder & Plane takes your project from design through construction. Denver metro. Full remodels from $75,000. Request a fit call to discuss your project.

Why these choices?

  1. 01 /

    Show the whole room.

    The comparison keeps the window and camera angle in view so the viewer can see what changed at the scale of a full remodel.

  2. 02 /

    Say what the studio does.

    The ads make the full-remodel offer, service area, and starting price clear before the viewer clicks.

  3. 03 /

    Compare two ways to show the work.

    Both ads use the same offer and next step. The proposed test compares two complete ad concepts. One shows a transformation and the other shows a different finished kitchen.

Proposed measurement.

Compare cost per qualified fit-call request for the two ad concepts. The studio would qualify requests by location, full-remodel scope, budget, and timing.

  • Fit-call requests and the share that meet the qualification criteria
  • Cost per qualified fit-call request and number of calls held
  • Paid design phases started after a fit call

Use qualified inquiries and held calls to decide which ad to keep. If the sample is too small, report the result as inconclusive.
