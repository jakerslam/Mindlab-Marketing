The brief

Start with
the real question.

Daybreak Studio is a fictional fitness business created for this demonstration. The brief: make an introductory program feel approachable to people who are curious about training but unsure where to start. This is an original sample, not client work.

01 /

Name the real hesitation.

Write for someone who wants to begin but worries they won't fit in. Keep the tone welcoming, specific, and free of transformation promises.

02 /

Connect the offer and the content.

Use the same message across a sample social post and an ad concept. Explain what a first session involves so the next step is easier to assess.

03 /

Plan the campaign before spending.

Choose one introductory offer, one local market, and one ad platform. Confirm the booking destination and measurement before launch.

What this demonstrates

This sample demonstrates a content and campaign direction. The visual is a presentation concept; design production is separately scoped. No campaign was run and no results are claimed.
