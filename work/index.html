A method you can use

Six questions.
A better starting brief.

Use these prompts before commissioning a brand plan, a piece of content, or a campaign. Bring the answers to the conversation.

  1. 01 / The offer

    What are we asking people to consider?

  2. 02 / The audience

    Who needs it, and what matters to them?

  3. 03 / The next step

    What should someone do after reading?

  4. 04 / The evidence

    Which reasons to believe can we support?

  5. 05 / The constraints

    What budget, inputs, and approvals shape the work?

  6. 06 / The review

    What will we measure, and who will act on it?
