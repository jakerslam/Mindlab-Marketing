Before we begin

Questions,
answered.

Final scope, prices, and terms are confirmed in your written agreement.

Do we need all three services?+

No. Start with the work that solves your immediate problem. If your positioning is already clear, you can begin with Content Studio or Demand Campaigns. We can review fit together before agreeing a scope.

Is advertising spend included?+

No. Demand Campaigns is a management fee. Your media budget is additional and paid through your advertising account. The package covers up to $10,000 in monthly media spend; larger budgets need a separate scope.

Does Content Studio include design or video?+

The listed package is writing-only. Graphics, photography, video, community management, and email distribution or automation are separately scoped.

How long is the engagement?+

Brand Blueprint is a project, usually taking about 2–3 weeks after inputs and interviews. Our proposed retainer terms start with 90 days, then continue monthly with 30 days' notice. Your written agreement confirms the final terms.

Who owns the accounts and work?+

Your business owns its advertising accounts and data. Ownership and transfer of deliverables follow the written agreement. We do not guarantee a number of leads, sales, or revenue.
