Privacy & Cookies

This page describes the tools enabled on this website and how information moves through them.

Who operates this site

Mindlab LLC operates this marketing website.

Information you choose to share

Our inquiry form is provided by Tally. It requires your full name, company name, and email address. Phone number and descriptions of your current brand strategy, advertising, and content creation are optional. We use responses to assess fit and respond to your inquiry. Avoid including sensitive information. Tally processes and stores submissions through its service. Tally privacy policy.

Scheduling

The call page includes a Cal.com calendar, and you can also open the calendar in a separate tab. Cal.com receives technical information when the calendar loads and handles the booking details you provide, including contact information and your chosen time. Review its privacy information before submitting.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies and other browser storage can remember preferences or support services you use on this site. Our own settings and the tools we embed are described below.

Optional analytics

Optional analytics are not enabled on this version of the website. We do not load Google Tag Manager or Google Analytics.

Display preferences

The footer lets you choose Light, System, or Dark. System follows your device’s appearance setting. We store your choice locally in your browser so it carries between pages and visits. This preference is not sent to analytics.

When you dismiss the cookie notice, we save that choice in your browser. Choosing Deny analytics also saves an opt-out for this browser if we enable optional analytics later.

Embedded tools

Cal.com loads on the booking page to display available times. Cal.com privacy information. Tally loads when you open the inquiry form. Tally privacy information. These providers may use their own cookies or browser storage. The optional analytics setting controls Mindlab’s analytics, and the embedded tools operate separately.

Technical information

Your browser sends technical information, such as an IP address and requested page, to the service hosting the website. That service may process access and security logs. External inquiry or scheduling tools receive technical information when their embeds load or you open their hosted pages.

Your choices

Share only the information needed for the conversation. You can choose whether to use external forms and booking tools.

Draft for review before launch. Business contact details, the hosting provider, enabled tools, and retention practices must be confirmed before this becomes the production privacy notice.
